## Interpretation and frozen selection

Both candidates improve equal-race MAE at 10 laps and finish, but neither passes
the finish-bias gate. A's bias is -5.421 s and B's -5.238 s versus pit+wear
-2.343 s. Therefore retain **pit+wear**, not the lower-MAE but disqualified B.
The signed bias interpretation also retains pit+wear, so the ambiguity does not
change the freeze. No parameters were retuned after seeing these results.

The ablation supports an MAE benefit from removing fitted-trend extrapolation;
it does not solve the optimistic bias. Bahrain A finish MAE improves
10.998 -> 10.074 s versus offsets, while Spain A mean finish bias worsens
-9.796 -> -10.323 s. B gives still lower equal-race MAE, but its pooled bias
remains too negative. Fixed fuel is not sufficient to separate all track,
compound and stint-management effects in these three races.

Frozen baseline limitations remain: Spain SOFT no-stop finish -1.067 s/lap,
no-stop finish Bahrain -0.278 and Spain -0.762 s/lap. Its opposing overall
finish biases (+7.270 Bahrain, -9.935 Spain) cancel in the pool. Green interval
coverage is only 52.35% at 10 laps and 37.94% at finish. This is a development
freeze, not validation of held-out/wet performance or strategy selection.
